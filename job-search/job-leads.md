## How to read this list

Job boards (LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor) don't expose individual postings or employer
emails to search engines, and most route applications through their own **Apply** button —
not email. So the leads below are split by how you actually apply:

- **📧 Email outreach possible** — the posting or company lets you reach a person directly.
  A tailored email lives in `emails/`.
- **🔘 Platform apply** — apply through the board's button; there's no address to email.
  Use the matching email in `emails/` as your cover letter / "message to hiring manager".

> Detail note: I could confirm titles and skill requirements from search results, but the
> boards blocked automated access to the full posting bodies, so **verify hours, pay, and
> current status on the live link before applying.**

set **Date posted = Past 24 hours/Week**, **Job type = Part-time**, **Remote**. Save the
search and turn on the alert so new posts hit your inbox daily.

## Applying from the Philippines to companies abroad — quick notes

- Lead with **time-zone overlap** you can offer (e.g. "I keep 4–5 hours overlap with US
  Eastern / UK hours") — it's the #1 concern for remote-hiring managers abroad.
- Say **"contractor / independent"** rather than "employee" — most foreign SMBs hire PH
  talent as contractors, which is simpler for them.
- State currency-agnostic availability ("open to hourly USD via Wise/Deel/Payoneer").
