# LinkedIn — Outreach & Application Messages

LinkedIn has no "email" step. You either **Easy Apply**, or you **connect + message** the
recruiter/hiring manager. Below are messages sized to LinkedIn's real limits, in your voice.
You send them yourself — I can't message from your account.

---

## A. Connection request note (max 300 characters)
Send when you find the recruiter/hiring manager on a part-time IT support post. Keep it short —
LinkedIn free accounts sometimes cap notes at ~200 chars, so this stays under that.

> Hi [First name] — saw your part-time IT support opening. I do M365 + Google Workspace
> admin and remote helpdesk, 10+ yrs, PH-based with US/UK-hours overlap. Would love to
> connect and share how I can help. — Maricris

*(198 characters. Swap "US/UK" for their actual region.)*

## E. Find newly posted part-time roles on LinkedIn (do this once, then let alerts work)

1. **LinkedIn → Jobs**, search each: `IT Support`, `Microsoft 365 Administrator`,
   `Help Desk`, `Google Workspace`.
2. Set filters: **Date posted = Past 24 hours** (or Past week) · **Job type = Part-time** ·
   **Remote** · Location = the country you're targeting (e.g. United States, United Kingdom).
3. Click **Set alert** (toggle) on each search — new posts then arrive in your inbox/notifications
   daily. This is the closest thing to "SEO for newly posted LinkedIn jobs."
4. **Boolean in the keyword box** to narrow fast:
   `("IT support" OR helpdesk OR "M365") AND (remote) AND ("part-time" OR contract)`
5. On a promising post, click **"Meet the hiring team"** or the poster's name → that's who
   you connect with using message A.

## F. Profile tweaks that make outreach land (5-minute wins)

- Turn on **#OpenToWork** → set job titles (IT Support Specialist, M365 Administrator,
  Help Desk), **Part-time + Contract**, **Remote**, and your target countries. Recruiters
  filter on exactly these.
- **Headline:** "Remote IT Support | Microsoft 365 & Google Workspace Admin | Helpdesk |
  10+ yrs | Open to part-time/contract" — keyword-rich so you show up in recruiter searches.
- Add your portfolio link (https://crisx28.github.io) to **Featured** and Contact info.
- Make sure "Microsoft 365", "Google Workspace", "Entra ID", "Help Desk", "Windows Server"
  are listed **Skills** — LinkedIn recruiter search is skill-keyword driven.

## G. Realities to plan around

- **You must send these** — I can't act inside your LinkedIn account.
- **Connection notes** need Premium to be guaranteed; free accounts get limited notes/month.
  If you're out of notes, connect without a note, then send message B once accepted.
- **InMail** (message C) needs Premium or the poster having "Open Profile."
- Don't mass-send identical notes — LinkedIn flags that. Change a few words each time
  (same rule as the email checklist).
