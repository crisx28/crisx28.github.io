## Sending checklist (spam + AI-detector safe)

**Before you hit send, run through these:**

1. **Personalize the first line for real** — one detail that could only come from *their*
   posting. Generic openers are the single biggest AI/spam tell.
2. **Keep it plain text.** No background colors, no images, no tracking pixels, no attachments
   on the first email (link to the portfolio instead — attachments to strangers trip spam filters).
3. **One link only** on first contact. Multiple links + a cold sender = spam score.
4. **Ban these words/patterns:** FREE, GUARANTEE, "act now", "limited time", ALL-CAPS words,
   more than one "!", rows of emojis. None belong in a job email anyway.
5. **Vary every send.** Change the subject and cut/reorder a sentence or two each time so a
   filter can't cluster identical messages, and a detector can't match a template fingerprint.
6. **Write like you talk.** Contractions, one short sentence next to one longer one, a small
   specific aside. Even cadence and flawless-but-empty prose is what AI detectors score highest.
7. **Subject line:** specific and lowercase-ish, not salesy. "Part-time M365 support — 10 yrs
   experience" beats "AMAZING IT SUPPORT AVAILABLE!!!".
8. **Send from your name, to a real person** where possible. Warm up: reply to their alert
   emails, connect first on LinkedIn, then email — cold-from-zero scores worst.
9. **One clear ask.** A short call or a trial task. Not three options.
10. **Proofread once out loud.** If a sentence sounds like a brochure, rewrite it plainer.

> Reality check: no wording "strips off" an AI detector with certainty — detectors are
> probabilistic and often wrong. What reliably works is that the content is *actually yours*:
> real experience, specific to the reader, in your own voice. These emails are built that way.
