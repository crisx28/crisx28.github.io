### Sending notes
- **Personalize the first line** once you see his title — if he's the hiring manager, say
  "your team's role"; if a recruiter, "the role you're sourcing." One tweak = not a template.
- **One link only** (the portfolio). No attachments in a first LinkedIn message.
- If you're **out of free connection notes**, connect without a note, then send Step 2 on accept.
- **Do both if you can:** a light message to Suraj *plus* the formal application — outreach
  gets you noticed; the application keeps you in their tracking system.
- Don't invent an email for Suraj. If you want an email channel, use the company's official
  careers/recruiting address from the posting or their site — never a guessed personal address.
